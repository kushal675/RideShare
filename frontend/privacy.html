Privacy Policy

How RideShare handles your information.

This page describes the information handled by the current version of RideShare, an independent student project.

Information you provide

When you create an account, RideShare collects your name, student email address, phone number, hostel block and password. You may also add your year and branch in your profile. Passwords are stored as hashes.

When you use the app, you may provide ride routes, dates, times and notes, group requests and invitations, and a WhatsApp group link. Please avoid putting sensitive information in ride notes.

How information is used and shared

RideShare uses this information to manage accounts, verify email addresses, help students find matches, coordinate groups and display notifications. Other users can see relevant information for matching and coordinating a group; be mindful of what you include in your requests and group links. If you use a WhatsApp link, your activity in WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp’s own policies.

Service providers and device storage

The app uses Supabase to store application data, Brevo to send verification emails, Render to run the backend, and Netlify to host the frontend. These services process information needed to provide their functions. RideShare also stores a login token and some profile details in your browser’s local storage to support your session.

Questions about your information
