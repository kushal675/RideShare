About RideShare

Travel together, more easily.

RideShare helps MAHE Bengaluru students find other students travelling along the same route around the same time. Create a ride request, explore matches, and connect with a group to coordinate your journey.

How it works

  1. Sign up and verify your email. Create an account using your learner.manipal.edu email address and the verification code sent to your inbox. Add the profile details needed to help you coordinate with other students.
  2. Create a ride request. Enter where you are travelling from and to, your travel date and time, and any helpful notes. You can manage your requests from the dashboard.
  3. Explore potential matches. Select your ride request to find students or groups travelling on a matching route and date around the same time. Choose the option that works for you.
  4. Connect with a group. Invite matching students to a group or request to join an existing one. Check your invitations, join requests and in-app notifications for responses.
  5. Make your plans together. Once connected, use the group details and any WhatsApp group link provided to agree on pickup, transport and how to share the cost. Students make these arrangements independently.

RideShare helps you find people for a journey; it does not automatically book a vehicle or make a payment. Keep an eye on your requests and notifications for updates.

An independent student project

RideShare is an independent student project and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by MAHE. It helps students connect; it does not book transport, arrange rides, collect fares, or process payments.
