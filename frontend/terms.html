RideShare

Terms of Use

A few ground rules for using RideShare.

RideShare is an independent student project that helps students connect with one another. By using it, you agree to use the service responsibly.

Using the service

  • Use your own eligible student email address and provide accurate information.
  • Keep your account credentials private. Do not impersonate anyone or misuse another person’s information.
  • Treat other students respectfully. Do not use RideShare to harass, threaten, spam, or mislead anyone.
  • Only share information and links that are relevant and appropriate for coordinating a ride.

Coordinating a ride

RideShare helps users find one another; users independently decide whether and how to travel together. RideShare does not book vehicles, verify drivers or users beyond the email verification process, arrange fares, or process payments. Discuss your plans with your group and use your own judgment when travelling.

Availability and changes

As a student project, RideShare may experience interruptions or change over time. Features and these terms may be updated as the project develops.
